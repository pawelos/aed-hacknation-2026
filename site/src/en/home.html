Problem

In sudden cardiac arrest every minute counts, and anyone can use an AED. But you need to know where the nearest one is, whether it is accessible and whether it works. Today's AED lists cannot promise that, because no one is responsible for them.

  • Scattered

    Several databases, each kept separately and in its own format.

  • Unverified

    Opening hours and contacts are missing. It is unclear who last checked a device, and when.

  • No one responsible

    Lists are kept by third parties, not by the owners. When the data is wrong, no one answers for it.
