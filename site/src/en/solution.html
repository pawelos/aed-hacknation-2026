A mechanism that collects data from every AED owner, checks it and publishes one open dataset. Every record names its source and reference scope.

  • Map and search

    The nearest AED by address or location, filtered by availability, status and type of place.

  • Owner panel

    Local governments, schools, companies and fire brigades add and update their devices in one form.

  • Validation and merging

    CSV, GeoJSON and API import, duplicate detection, checks of coordinates, addresses and dates.

  • Export to dane.gov.pl

    Open CSV and GeoJSON with metadata that follows the rules of the dane.gov.pl portal.

How it works

  1. CollectData from AED owners and existing databases, with the source of every record.
  2. CheckCoordinates, addresses, hours and verification dates; duplicates are merged into one record.
  3. PublishOne dataset on dane.gov.pl, kept up to date by the owners themselves.
